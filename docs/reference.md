# Climate Hub reference

Lookup tables for the entities, attributes, settings, repairs and diagnostics of Climate Hub. Names are the
English UI names from `translations/en.json`; raw states, reasons, keys and attributes are in backticks. The tables
come from the integration code. For setup and a first walk-through see [`../README.md`](../README.md); for how the
values are computed see [`logic.md`](logic.md).

Entity IDs are generated by Home Assistant from area, device name and entity name, in the language of the instance.
They are not listed here; the device page of a room, outdoor side, window or the hub shows them.

## Object model

- One hub config entry (only one per instance). Its device is named "Home climate" and carries the hub entities.
- Subentries of the hub, each with one device: `room` (room), `region` (outdoor side) and `window` (window).
- A room belongs to one area and has an optional main side (North, East, South, West or None). Its device is named
  "Climate" (or "{area} climate" when the entity ID does not contain the area).
- An outdoor side belongs to one compass direction and one outdoor area. Each direction has at most one outdoor side.
  Its device is named like a room device.
- A window belongs to one area. It is linked to the room of that area; without that room it still reports its state
  (see `windows_without_room` under [Repairs](#repairs)). Its device is named after the window, with the area in front
  when the entity ID does not contain it.

Entities that the configuration does not produce are not created and are removed from the entity registry. An entity
is `unavailable` while the last evaluation produced no result for its room, outdoor side, window or the hub.

Measurement sensors (all numeric ones above) have state class `measurement`. Values are rounded to 2 decimals
(temperatures, absolute humidity, dew point, differences) or 1 decimal (humidity, pressure).

Created when:

- `temperature`, `comfort`, `trend`, `recommendation`: always. `trend` is `unknown` until 30 min of
  temperature samples exist.
- `humidity`, `absolute_humidity`, `dew_point`: the room has a humidity source (its own list, or the devices of the
  temperature sources when the list is empty). `humidity_difference` also needs an outdoor side or a weather service.
- `pressure`: the room has a pressure source (same rule).
- `outside_temperature`, `outside_humidity`, `temperature_difference`, `outside`: at least one outdoor side or a
  weather service is configured.
- `window`, `window_open_since`, `cooling_down`, `heating_up`: the room has at least one window.
- `open_while_conditioning`: the room has at least one window and at least one heating or cooling device.

Meaning of the room-specific states:

- `outside` compares the outside temperature at the room's main side with the room temperature: `colder` at a
  difference of -0.5 °C or lower, `warmer` at +0.5 °C or higher, otherwise `similar`; a state is kept until the
  difference passes 0.25 °C.
- `comfort` compares the room temperature with the comfort range (see [Writable numbers](#writable-numbers)), with a
  hysteresis of 0.3 °C.
- `trend` is the slope of the room temperature over the last 60 min: `rising` from +0.5 K/h, `falling` from -0.5 K/h,
  kept until 0.25 K/h.
- `window` is the most open window of the room; the window states are explained under
  [Window entities](#window-entities).
- `window_open_since` is the time the earliest ventilating window of the room started ventilating.
- `cooling_down` is on when a window has ventilated longer than the burst duration, the daily strategy is
  `prevent_cool_down`, outside is colder, and the recommendation does not ask for an open window. `heating_up` is the
  mirror case: strategy `prevent_heat_up`, outside warmer or room temperature `rising`.
- `open_while_conditioning` is on when a window has ventilated longer than the burst duration while a heating or
  cooling device of the room is active (`hvac_action` is `heating`, `preheating`, `cooling` or `drying`; without an
  action, `hvac_mode` is one of `heat`, `cool`, `heat_cool`, `auto`, `dry`).
- The three binary sensors are `unknown` while the window state is unknown.

## Outdoor side entities

Device of an outdoor side. The values are the ones the side's own sources deliver, or the next stage of the fallback
chain when they fail (see `stage` under [Provenance attributes](#provenance-attributes)). All entities are enabled by
default and always created.

| Name | `translation_key` | Platform | Unit / device class | Raw states (English label) | Attributes | Enabled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | `temperature` | sensor | °C / `temperature` | number | `source`, `stage`, `regions`, `reason`, `offset` | yes |
| Humidity | `humidity` | sensor | % / `humidity` | number | `source`, `stage`, `regions`, `reason`, `offset` | yes |
| Pressure | `pressure` | sensor | hPa / `atmospheric_pressure` | number | `source`, `stage`, `regions`, `reason`, `offset` | yes |
| Absolute humidity | `absolute_humidity` | sensor | g/m³ / `absolute_humidity` | number | `stage`, `regions`, `reason` | yes |
| Dew point | `dew_point` | sensor | °C / `temperature` | number | `stage`, `regions`, `reason` | yes |

`source` appears only while the side's own sources deliver the value (`stage` is `primary` or `fallback`). `offset`
appears only while `stage` is `weather`; `absolute_humidity` and `dew_point` never carry it. All five have state class
`measurement`.

## Window entities

Device of a window.

| Name | `translation_key` | Platform | Unit / device class | Raw states (English label) | Attributes | Enabled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| State | `state` | sensor | `enum` | `closed`: closed; `ajar`: ajar; `tilted`: tilted; `open`: open | `open_leaves` | yes |
| Open since | `open_since` | sensor | `timestamp` | timestamp | none | yes |
| Cover | `cover` | sensor | `enum` | `close`: close; `keep_closed`: keep closed; `open`: open; `keep_open`: keep open; `none`: none | `reason` | only with a cover |

- `closed`: no contact reports open. `ajar`: the sash contact is closed and the handle contact reports the handle
  turned. `tilted`: the sash is open but not turned fully open (tilt contact closed), or the text sensor reports
  tilted. `open`: the sash is turned fully open. A pass-through shows the lower of its two openings. The state is
  `unknown` while no contact delivers a usable value, and on a double leaf while one leaf is unknown and the other is
  below `tilted`. After the first evaluation, the state changes only after the contacts have been stable for 5 s.
  See [`logic.md`](logic.md#8-module-window-w2w12-scenarios-58).
- `open_leaves` exists only on a double leaf window. Values: `none`: none; `left`: left; `right`: right; `both`:
  both; `unknown`: unknown (a leaf contact is unknown, or the tilt contact reports open while no leaf does).
- `open_since` is the time the window started ventilating, that is, reached the level set in
  `min_level` ("Counts as ventilating from", default `tilted`). It is empty while the window does not ventilate.
- `cover` is a recommendation for the cover of the window; Climate Hub never moves a cover. The entity is created for
  every window and enabled by default only on a window with a cover set; for the others it is disabled. Adding a cover
  to a window enables the entity. Reasons: see [Cover reasons](#cover-reasons).
- Advice mapping: when the cover should be closed, `close` if it is not closed and `keep_closed` if it is (position
  50 % or lower; for a cover without a position, state `closed`; `opening`, `closing`, `unavailable` and `unknown`
  count as unknown position); when it should be open, `open` if it is closed or its position is unknown and
  `keep_open` if it is not closed; `none` when there is no wish.

## Hub entities

Device "Home climate". All entities are enabled by default.

| Name | `translation_key` | Platform | Unit / device class | Raw states (English label) | Attributes | Enabled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily strategy | `strategy` | sensor | `enum` | `prevent_heat_up`: Prevent heating up; `prevent_cool_down`: Prevent cooling down; `neutral`: neutral | `forecast_max`, `forecast_min`, `corrected_max`, `corrected_min`, `bias_max`, `bias_min` | yes |
| Indoor average | `indoor_average` | sensor | °C / `temperature` | number | `minimum`, `maximum` | yes |
| Outdoor | `outdoor` | sensor | °C / `temperature` | number | `stage`, `minimum`, `maximum`, `per_region` | yes |
| Comfort temperature min | `comfort_min` | number | °C / `temperature` | number | none | yes |
| Comfort temperature max | `comfort_max` | number | °C / `temperature` | number | none | yes |
| Threshold | `threshold` | number | °C / `temperature_delta` | number | none | yes |
| Anywhere cooling down | `any_cooling_down` | binary_sensor | none | `on` / `off` | none | yes |
| Anywhere heating up | `any_heating_up` | binary_sensor | none | `on` / `off` | none | yes |
| Anywhere open while heating/cooling | `any_open_while_conditioning` | binary_sensor | none | `on` / `off` | none | yes |
| Anywhere cover to close | `any_cover_to_close` | binary_sensor | none | `on` / `off` | `windows` | yes |

Created when:

- `strategy`: a weather service is set in the hub options.
- `indoor_average`: at least one room exists.
- `outdoor`: at least one outdoor side exists or a weather service is set.
- `any_cooling_down`, `any_heating_up`, `any_cover_to_close`: at least one window exists.
- `any_open_while_conditioning`: at least one room has windows and heating or cooling devices.
- The numbers: always. Their ranges are under [Writable numbers](#writable-numbers).

Details:

- `indoor_average` is the mean of the room temperatures that have a value; `minimum` and `maximum` are the lowest and
  highest of them (°C).
- `outdoor` is the mean of the temperatures of all outdoor sides that have a value, from their own sources. `stage` is
  `fallback` if any of those sides uses a lower-ranked source, otherwise `primary`; without any side value the
  corrected weather temperature is used (`weather`), or no value (`none`). `per_region` maps each direction (`n`, `e`,
  `s`, `w`) to its temperature in °C; it is absent when the weather service supplies the value. `per_region` and
  `offset` are excluded from the recorder.
- `any_cooling_down`, `any_heating_up`, `any_open_while_conditioning` are on when any room has the matching binary
  sensor on, `unknown` when none is on and at least one is unknown.
- `any_cover_to_close` is on when at least one window has the cover advice `close` or `keep_closed`. `windows` lists
  those windows as `entity_id` (the window's `cover` sensor) and `reason` (a cover reason, raw value).

## Provenance attributes

Measurement sensors say where their value came from. An attribute without a value is not written.

| Attribute | Name in the UI | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `source` | Source | Entity ID of the source in use. |
| `stage` | Stage | Where the value comes from, see the table below. |
| `regions` | Outdoor sides | Directions (`n`, `e`, `s`, `w`) whose values were averaged into an outside value. Absent for `weather` and `none`. |
| `reason` | Reason | Why the first source is not in use (own measurements), or why an outside value is missing, see below. |
| `offset` | Offset | Correction applied to the value of a lower-ranked source or of the weather service, in the unit of the value, rounded to 2 decimals. Excluded from the recorder. |

Which attributes appear:

- Room `temperature`, `humidity`, `pressure`: `source` and `stage` always (when a value exists); `reason` only when
  `stage` is not `primary`; `offset` only when `stage` is `fallback`.
- Room `absolute_humidity` and `dew_point`: the attributes of the weaker of their two inputs (temperature and
  humidity; order `primary`, `fallback`, `none`; a tie goes to humidity), without `offset`.
- Room `outside_temperature` and `outside_humidity` and the outdoor side sensors: `stage`, `regions`, `reason` (only
  at `none`), `offset` (only at `weather`), and `source` on outdoor side sensors.

`stage` values:

| Raw value | English label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `primary` | primary source | The first source of the list delivers a valid value. |
| `fallback` | fallback source | A lower-ranked source of the same list delivers; its learned typical deviation from the first source is subtracted (`offset`). |
| `neighbour` | neighbouring side | Outside values: the own outdoor side has no value; the mean of the adjacent outdoor sides is used. |
| `opposite` | opposite side | Outside values: neither the own nor the adjacent sides have a value; the opposite side is used. |
| `weather` | weather service | Outside values: no outdoor side has a value; the weather service value is used, corrected by its learned deviation (`offset`). |
| `none` | no value | No stage delivers a value; `reason` says why. |

`reason` values:

| Raw value | English label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ok` | ok | Defined as a status of a source. It is never written as `reason`: a `reason` exists only when the first source is not usable. |
| `unavailable` | unavailable | The source entity is `unavailable`. |
| `unknown` | unknown | The source entity is `unknown`, or has no value. |
| `not_numeric` | not numeric | The state of the source is not a number. |
| `unit` | wrong unit | The unit cannot be converted: temperature to °C, pressure to hPa; humidity must be %. |
| `missing` | missing | The source entity does not exist in Home Assistant. |
| `stale` | stale | The source reported a value, but not within the maximum age of a measurement (default 40 min, `max_age`). |
| `no_side` | no outdoor side | Outside values of a room without a main side. |
| `no_pair` | no matching pair | Outside absolute humidity and dew point: no stage has a temperature and a humidity value from the same sides. |
| `no_data` | no data | Outside temperature, humidity or pressure: no stage, including the weather service, delivers a value. |

The first seven values come from the status of the first source. The last three are used only for outside values at
stage `none`. The fusion rules are in [`logic.md`](logic.md#4-module-sources-r2-r4-r5-r7-r8-o2-o5-e6) and
[`logic.md`](logic.md#5-module-outdoor-o1o7-adr-003-scenarios-3-and-4).

## Recommendation reasons

The attribute `reason` of the room `recommendation` sensor. The state is derived from the wish and the window state: a
wish for an open window gives `open`, or `keep_open` while the room's windows ventilate; a wish for a closed window
gives `keep_closed` when no window ventilates, otherwise `close`. `none` goes with `no_side`, `no_data` and
`comfort_range_invalid`. Rules in [`logic.md`](logic.md#9-module-recommendation-a2a6-a12-scenarios-9-11-12).

Terms used below: the outside is `colder` or `warmer` as in the room sensor `outside`. Outside air is "drier" when its
absolute humidity is at least 1.0 g/m³ lower than inside (kept until 0.5 g/m³). The humidity threshold, burst duration
and comfort range come from [Settings](#settings) and [Writable numbers](#writable-numbers).

| Raw value | English label | When it occurs |
|---|---|---|
| `cool_room` | cool the room | Outside is colder and the room is above the comfort range; wish: open until the room reaches the middle of the range. |
| `pre_cool` | pre-cool | As `cool_room`, on a day with strategy `prevent_heat_up`: also starts when the room is 0.5 °C or more above the comfort minimum, and cools down to the comfort minimum. |
| `warm_room` | warm the room | Outside is warmer and the room is below the comfort range; wish: open until the room reaches the middle of the range. |
| `dry_room` | dry the room | Room humidity is above the humidity threshold, outside air is drier, and opening does not harm the temperature; wish: open until humidity falls 5 points below the threshold. |
| `burst_dry` | burst ventilation to dry | As `dry_room`, but opening would harm the temperature (outside colder on a `prevent_cool_down` day or with the room below its range; outside warmer on a `prevent_heat_up` day or with the room above its range); wish: open for the burst duration (default 10 min). |
| `burst_done` | burst ventilation done | The burst duration has passed and the room is still humid; wish: closed. The next burst starts 1 h after the window was closed (without windows: 1 h after the end of the burst). |
| `outside_colder` | outside colder | No wish to open, and opening would cool the room too much (outside colder with strategy `prevent_cool_down` or room below its range). |
| `outside_warmer` | outside warmer | No wish to open, and opening would heat the room too much (outside warmer with strategy `prevent_heat_up` or room above its range). |
| `outside_humid` | outside more humid | A temperature goal exists, but ventilating would bring the room humidity to the humidity threshold or above (released 5 points below it). |
| `in_range` | within the comfort range | No wish to open and the room is within the comfort range. |
| `no_benefit` | no benefit | No wish to open and none of the reasons above applies. |
| `no_data` | no data | The room temperature or the outside temperature has no value. |
| `no_side` | no outdoor side | The room has no main side (None). |
| `comfort_range_invalid` | comfort range invalid | The comfort minimum is not below the comfort maximum. |

## Cover reasons

The attribute `reason` of the window `cover` sensor, and `reason` inside the `windows` attribute of
`any_cover_to_close`. Checked in the order of the table, the first match wins; `window_open` is applied last and
replaces a held "close" wish. Rules in
[`logic.md`](logic.md#10-module-cover-sh1-sh2-sh4-d16).

Terms used below: "sun on the window" means the sun is at least 10° above the horizon, within 60° of the window's
facing (`facing_degrees`, else the compass side of the window) and the cloud cover, when known, is 70 % or lower. A
night is a sun elevation below 0°. "Heat risk" means the daily strategy is `prevent_heat_up` or the room temperature
is within 0.5 °C of the comfort maximum (kept until 1.0 °C below it). A switch between "close" and "open" waits until
the current wish has lasted 15 min; until then the previous wish and its reason stay. Switches from or to "none" take
effect at once.

| Raw value | English label | When it occurs | Wish |
|---|---|---|---|
| `no_room` | no room | The window has no room in its area. | none |
| `no_data` | no data | The sun position or the room temperature has no value, the window has no facing (Facing "Like the room" in a room without a main side, no degrees set), or the room's evaluation failed. | none |
| `insulate` | cold at night, keep warmth in | Night, outside colder, and the strategy is `prevent_cool_down` or the room is below its comfort range. | close |
| `night` | night, no reason to close | Night, and `insulate` does not apply. | none |
| `solar_gain` | solar warmth welcome | Sun on the window and the room is below its comfort range, or the outside temperature is below the comfort minimum. | open |
| `sun_heat` | sun on the window, keep heat out | Sun on the window and heat risk. | close |
| `heat_barrier` | hotter outside, keep heat out | Strategy `prevent_heat_up`, outside warmer, and solar warmth is not welcome. | close |
| `no_heat_risk` | sun, no overheating | Sun on the window, no heat risk. | open |
| `no_sun` | no sun on the window | Daytime, none of the above. | open |
| `window_open` | window open | The wish would be "close", but the window state is `open`; the cover is not asked to close in front of an open window. | none |

## Daily strategy

The sensor `strategy` ("Daily strategy") says whether the day calls for keeping heat out or keeping warmth in. It is
created only when a weather service is set in the hub options.

| Raw state | English label | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| `prevent_heat_up` | Prevent heating up | The corrected forecast maximum is above the comfort maximum plus the threshold. Kept until it is 0.5 °C below that limit. |
| `prevent_cool_down` | Prevent cooling down | The corrected forecast minimum is below the comfort minimum minus the threshold. Kept until it is 0.5 °C above that limit. |
| `neutral` | neutral | Neither applies. |

`prevent_heat_up` is checked first. The sensor is `unknown` without a forecast: the weather service must provide a
forecast through `weather.get_forecasts` (hourly preferred, else daily). An hourly forecast needs at least 12 points
in the next 24 h; a daily forecast needs an entry for today. A failed fetch keeps the last forecast. The forecast is
requested when the weather entity changes, at most every 15 min, and at least every 60 min.

Attributes, all in °C and rounded to 2 decimals, written when they have a value:

| Attribute | Name in the UI | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `forecast_max` | Forecast maximum | Highest forecast temperature: next 24 h (hourly) or today's high (daily). |
| `forecast_min` | Forecast minimum | Lowest forecast temperature (daily: today's low, when the service provides it). |
| `corrected_max` | Corrected forecast maximum | `forecast_max` plus `bias_max`. |
| `corrected_min` | Corrected forecast minimum | `forecast_min` plus `bias_min`. |
| `bias_max` | Forecast bias maximum | Learned average difference between the house's outdoor sensors and the forecast maximum, limited to ±5 °C. |
| `bias_min` | Forecast bias minimum | Same for the minimum. |

The bias is learned once per day, from days with at least 18 h of outdoor sensor coverage, the first day setting it to
the difference and each later day moving it by 25 % of the new difference. It is applied to the forecast only after 3
such days; before that, `corrected_max` equals `forecast_max`. Rules in
[`logic.md`](logic.md#7-module-strategy-a1-a14-d4-d15).

## Writable numbers

The `number` entities of the hub device. A change is stored at once and used by the next evaluation. A value that would
make the comfort minimum equal to or higher than the comfort maximum is rejected with the error "Comfort minimum
({min} °C) must be below comfort maximum ({max} °C)." and the old value stays.

| Name | `translation_key` | Unit | Min | Max | Step | Default |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Comfort temperature min | `comfort_min` | °C | 10 | 30 | 0.5 | 20 |
| Comfort temperature max | `comfort_max` | °C | 15 | 35 | 0.5 | 24 |
| Threshold | `threshold` | °C | 0.5 | 10 | 0.5 | 2 |

The comfort range is shared by all rooms. `threshold` is the distance beyond the comfort range at which the forecast
switches the daily strategy (see [Daily strategy](#daily-strategy)).

## Settings

### Hub options

Open them with the gear icon ("Configure") next to the Climate Hub entry, not with "System options" in its three-dot
menu. The same two first fields are asked during setup.

| Label | Key | Type | Default | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weather service | `weather` | weather entity, optional | the first weather entity with a forecast, at setup | | Provides the forecast for the daily strategy and the outside values when all outdoor sensors fail. Without it, the daily strategy is not created. |
| Keep areas up to date | `area_sync` | switch | on | | Enters the room and outdoor side `temperature` and `humidity` sensors as the temperature and humidity sensor of their areas. An area value that you changed afterwards is left alone; entries are cleared again when the sync is turned off or the integration is removed. |
| Maximum age of a measurement | `max_age` | number, minutes | 40 | 1 to 1440, step 1 | A reading older than this counts as failed (`stale`) and the next source takes over. Rooms and outdoor sides can override it. |
| Duration of burst ventilation | `burst_duration` | number, minutes | 10 | 1 to 60, step 1 | How long a burst of fresh air lasts. The warnings `cooling_down`, `heating_up` and `open_while_conditioning` start only after a window has ventilated this long. |
| Ventilate for humidity from | `humidity_threshold` | number, % | 60 | 30 to 90, step 1 | From this room humidity the recommendation asks for ventilation if the outside air is drier. Also the limit for the humidity guard (`outside_humid`). |
| Report an outage after | `repair_delay` | number, hours | 6 | 1 to 72, step 1 | How long a source must stay without a valid value before an outage repair appears. |

The last four fields are in the collapsed section "Times and thresholds". If the chosen weather service is
unavailable or offers no forecast, a menu follows with "Save anyway" and "Adjust".

### House plan

Set in step 3 of the first setup and changed with "Reconfigure" in the three-dot menu of the Climate Hub entry. One
section per direction (North, East, South, West):

| Label | Key | Type | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outdoor area | `outdoor` | area, optional | empty | The area whose sensors measure this side. An area serves one side only. Empty: no outdoor side for this direction; the fallback chain and the weather service stand in. |
| Rooms on this side | `rooms` | rooms, optional | empty | The rooms whose windows face this side; it becomes their main side. A room belongs to one side only. |

Saving creates, changes or removes the outdoor sides and sets the main side of the rooms. A new or changed outdoor area
shows a preview with the suggested sources first.

### Room form

Add a room with "Add room" (first the area, then this form); change it with "Reconfigure room". A room needs an area
that is not already a room and not an outdoor side.

| Label | Key | Type | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Area | `area` | area | chosen in the first step | The area the room stands for. Changing it suggests the sources anew; sources without an area stay. |
| Main side | `side` | North, East, South, West, None | None | The outdoor side the outside air comes from when ventilating here. Interior rooms: None; a room without a side has no recommendation (`no_side`). |
| Temperature sources, most important first | `temperature` | entity list, ordered, at least 1 | suggested from the area | If the top source fails, the next one takes over; its typical deviation from the top source is compensated. |
| Humidity sources | `humidity` | entity list, ordered, optional | empty | Section "Humidity and pressure". Empty means the humidity entities of the same devices as the temperature sources. |
| Pressure sources | `pressure` | entity list, ordered, optional | empty | Same section and rule as humidity. |
| Heating and cooling devices | `climate_devices` | climate entities, optional | suggested from the area | Section "Heating and cooling". Only read, never controlled; used for `open_while_conditioning`. |
| Maximum age of a measurement | `max_age` | number, minutes | empty (hub value) | Section "Advanced". 1 to 1440. Overrides the hub option for this room. |

### Outdoor side form

Add with "Add outdoor side" (first direction and area, then this form); change with "Reconfigure outdoor side".

| Label | Key | Type | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | `direction` | North, East, South, West | chosen in the first step | Compass direction of the side. Only directions without an outdoor side are offered. |
| Outdoor area | `area` | area | chosen in the first step | The area whose sensors measure this side. It must not be a room or another outdoor side. Changing it suggests the sources anew. |
| Temperature sources, most important first | `temperature` | entity list, ordered, at least 1 | suggested from the area | As for a room. |
| Humidity sources | `humidity` | entity list, ordered, optional | empty | Section "Humidity and pressure". Empty means the same devices as the temperature sources. |
| Pressure sources | `pressure` | entity list, ordered, optional | empty | Same section and rule. |
| Maximum age of a measurement | `max_age` | number, minutes | empty (hub value) | Section "Advanced". 1 to 1440. |

### Window form

Add with "Add window" (room, then type, then this form); change with "Reconfigure window". The type is one of
"Single window or door", "Double leaf" and "Pass-through" (ventilating only works when two openings in a row are
open). The fields depend on the type.

| Label | Key | Types | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Room | `area` | all | chosen in the first step | The area of the window. It decides which contacts are offered and which room the window belongs to. |
| Contact | `leaf` | single | none (required) | The contact that reports whether the window is open. |
| Left leaf / Right leaf | `leaf_left`, `leaf_right` | double | none (both required) | The contacts of the two leaves. |
| Contact between the leaves (optional) | `tilt` | double | none | Tells tilted from open: it reports open only when a leaf is turned fully open. |
| Inner opening / Outer opening | `inner`, `outer` | pass-through | none (both required) | The opening in this room and the opening to the outside. The outer list shows all contacts. |
| Show all contacts | `show_all` | all | off | Lists every contact in the house instead of those of the area and without an area. |
| Name | `name` | all | empty | Empty gives "{area} Window" (pass-through: "{area} Passage"). |
| Tilt detection | `tilt` | single | none | Section "Extra contacts". A second contact on the sash that reports open only when the sash is turned fully open. |
| Handle contact | `handle` | single, double | none | Section "Extra contacts". Reports whether the handle is turned; with the sash closed and the handle turned the state is `ajar`. |
| Cover | `cover` | all | none | Section "Cover". The cover entity in front of the window. Creates the recommendation in `cover`. |
| Facing | `facing` | all | `room` (Like the room) | Section "Advanced". Like the room, North, East, South or West. Decides which outdoor side and which sun apply. |
| Exact facing in degrees | `facing_degrees` | all | empty | Section "Advanced". 0 to 360, step 1, North 0, East 90, South 180, West 270. Used for the sun angle. |
| Counts as ventilating from | `min_level` | all | `tilted` (Tilted) | Section "Advanced". Ajar, Tilted or Open: from this state on the window counts as ventilating. |

Contacts are binary sensors of device class window, door, opening or garage door (`on` means open), or text sensors.
For a text sensor an extra step "Assign states" asks which texts mean `closed`, `ajar`, `tilted` and `open`; matching
is case-insensitive on the whole text and a text in none of the four lists is unknown.

## Repairs

Climate Hub raises up to three kinds of repair issues, all with severity warning. They are not persistent: after a
restart they are created again when the condition still holds.

| Issue (`translation_key`) | Raised when | Fixing | Disappears when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `outage` | A source of a room or outdoor side has had no valid value for the "Report an outage after" time (hub option `repair_delay`, default 6 h), another source is in use, and the correction applied to it is not zero (rounded to 1 decimal). | Not fixable from the issue; check the battery or the connection. | The source delivers a valid value again, the source is removed from the list, or the room or outdoor side is deleted. |
| `outage_fallback` | As `outage`, with another source in use and no correction. | As `outage`. | As `outage`. |
| `outage_none` | As `outage`, but no source of the failed metric (temperature, humidity or pressure) of that object has a value. | As `outage`. | As `outage`. |
| `new_source` | A sensor or climate entity of the area of a room or outdoor side qualifies as a temperature source and is neither in the list nor seen before. Checked after the first evaluation at start and 5 s after the last change to the entity, device or area registry. One issue per entity. | A menu: "Add as a source" appends it as the last temperature source (and, when the humidity or pressure list is not empty, the humidity or pressure entities of its device); "Do not add" ignores the issue, as Home Assistant's own "Ignore" does. | The entity is added, is no longer a candidate, or the room or outdoor side is deleted. An ignored issue stays ignored; undo with "Show ignored repairs". |
| `windows_without_room` | A window exists in an area that has no room. Checked at setup and after each configuration change. One issue per area. | A menu: "Recreate the room" shows a preview and creates the room with suggested sources; "Delete the windows" removes the windows of the area and their entities. | The area has a room again or the windows are deleted. |

The three outage variants share the same title, "Sensor for {title} not responding"; they differ in the text: `outage`
names the active replacement and its correction, `outage_fallback` names the replacement only, `outage_none` states that
there is no replacement. A source counts as not responding in every status except `ok`, `stale` included.

## Diagnostics

The download on the Climate Hub entry ("Download diagnostics" in its three-dot menu) is one JSON file; an admin token
gets the same file from `GET /api/diagnostics/config_entry/<entry_id>`. Home Assistant puts its own keys at the top
(`home_assistant`, `custom_components`, `integration_manifest`, `setup_times`, and `issues` with the repairs) and
Climate Hub's decision chain under `data`:

- `options`: the hub options.
- `subentries`: ID, type, title and data of every room, outdoor side and window.
- `model`: the configuration as the logic uses it (sources after "empty means follow", windows linked to rooms).
- `state`: the persisted engine state (learned offsets, goals, hysteresis states, forecast bias, outage timers).
- `comfort`: comfort minimum, maximum and threshold.
- `results`: the last evaluation result for `rooms`, `regions`, `windows` and `house`, or `null` before the first one.
- `failures`: objects that failed to evaluate in the last run.
- `forecast`: the last fetched forecast.
- `store`: bookkeeping: the entities seen per room and outdoor side (for `new_source`), the area sync records and the
  device areas.

Nothing is redacted. The file contains entity IDs, area IDs, names of rooms, outdoor sides and windows, and the
measured values; check it before posting it publicly.
